# The Lost Lead Recovery Sprint

A defensible 72-hour service sprint for HealthAxis Consulting to help local healthcare and healthcare-adjacent organizations recover booked appointments from inquiries they already paid to acquire.

This offer is designed for regulated environments where missed follow-up, unanswered reviews, unclear booking paths, and dormant inquiries can create both revenue leakage and operational risk. The service does not replace clinical judgment, compliance review, or authorized patient communication workflows.

## Executive Summary

Most local organizations focus on generating new demand. The Lost Lead Recovery Sprint focuses on recovering value from demand that already exists.

The core promise:

> Within 72 hours, HealthAxis identifies where leads are being lost, documents the follow-up gaps, writes compliant response scripts, and delivers a simple workflow the client can review, approve, and use immediately.

Initial offer:

- **$500 Lost Lead Recovery Sprint** for first clients or pilot accounts.
- **72-hour delivery window** after intake materials are received.
- **No ad spend, public posting schedule, inventory, or personal brand requirement.**
- **Human approval required** before client staff use scripts, sequences, or review responses.

Retainer path:

- **$750 to $1,250 per month** for ongoing lead leak monitoring, script refreshes, dormant lead campaign support, review response drafting, and KPI reporting.
- Ten clients at $1,000 per month creates a clear $10,000 monthly recurring revenue target.

## 72-Hour Execution Plan

### Day 1: Build the List and Identify Public Leaks

Choose one niche in one metro area.

Use Google Maps, public websites, and Google Business Profiles to identify 30 prospects. Do not scrape private data, impersonate patients, or access systems without permission.

Look for visible leakage indicators:

- Recent reviews with no owner response
- Negative reviews with no documented response
- Contact forms with weak confirmation language
- Booking paths that require too many steps
- No visible missed-call text-back process
- Confusing phone, form, or appointment calls to action
- Evidence that ads, forms, or lead magnets are generating inquiries without a clear follow-up path

Tracker fields:

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | Prospect identification |
| Owner or manager | Decision-maker target |
| Email | Outreach channel |
| Phone | Public business phone |
| Website | Lead path review |
| Public leak observed | Specific, evidence-based outreach hook |
| Likely value per lead | Prioritization estimate |
| Outreach sent | Accountability checkpoint |
| Follow-up date | Repeatable next action |
| Status | Pipeline visibility |

Create five short Loom audits. Each should be three to five minutes and show:

1. One public leak
2. Why it may cost booked appointments
3. One fix
4. The $500 sprint offer
5. An invitation to send the one-page outline

### Day 2: Send Direct Outreach

Send 20 specific emails. Do not send deceptive, generic, or high-pressure messages.

Subject line options:

- Found one quiet leak on your website
- Quick idea for recovering more appointments
- I noticed this in your lead flow
- You may already have leads you can recover

Email template:

```text
Hi [Name],

I was looking at [Business Name] and noticed one place where inquiries may be slipping away before they ever book: [specific observation].

That is usually less of a marketing problem and more of a follow-up problem. Many local healthcare and service businesses pay to generate interest, then lose part of the return through missed calls, slow replies, unanswered reviews, or no reactivation process for older inquiries.

I recorded a quick 3-minute audit showing what I found and the first fix I would make:
[Loom link]

I also offer a 72-hour Lost Lead Recovery Sprint. I map the leaks, write the follow-up scripts, and hand over a simple workflow your team can review and use right away. The flat pilot fee is $500.

Would you like me to send the one-page outline?

Best,
[Name]
[Company]
[Valid postal address]
[Clear opt-out instruction]
```

Compliance checkpoint:

- Identify the sender honestly.
- Avoid deceptive subject lines.
- Include a valid postal address.
- Include a clear opt-out method.
- Do not use unauthorized automated SMS outreach.
- Do not imply a clinical, legal, or regulatory conclusion from public observations alone.

### Day 3: Follow Up and Close

Follow-up 1:

```text
Hi [Name], circling back in case this got buried. The short version is that I found a couple of places where [Business Name] may be losing inquiries you already paid to generate. Happy to send the one-page outline if useful.
```

Follow-up 2:

```text
Last note from me, [Name]. I will close the loop after this. If recovering even one or two missed bookings a month would be worth looking at, I can send the audit outline over.
```

When the prospect says yes, send:

- One-page scope
- Payment link
- Simple intake form
- Delivery date within 72 hours
- Approval note explaining that all scripts and workflows are drafts until approved by the client

Avoid long proposals. The offer should stay small, specific, and easy to accept.

## Delivery Package

### 1. Revenue Leak Snapshot

Include:

- Where leads appear to be dropping
- What each leak may cost operationally or financially
- Top three fixes in priority order
- Evidence source for each observation
- Whether the finding is public observation, client-provided information, or assumption

### 2. Script Pack

Include draft language for:

- Missed-call text
- Missed-call voicemail
- Form inquiry follow-up
- No-response follow-up
- Dormant lead reactivation
- Review response templates

Each script should include:

- Intended use case
- Communication channel
- Required approval owner
- Consent check if SMS or marketing email is involved
- Date approved
- Date last reviewed

### 3. Simple Workflow

Document who responds and when.

Minimum workflow checkpoints:

| Timeframe | Required action | Review point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 minutes | First response attempt or internal assignment | Confirm lead source and urgency |
| 24 hours | Second follow-up attempt | Confirm no duplicate or closed inquiry |
| 3 days | Value-based follow-up | Confirm message is appropriate and approved |
| 7 days | Dormant lead reactivation or close-out | Confirm consent and status before further outreach |

### 4. Tracking Sheet

Recommended fields:

- Lead name
- Source
- Date received
- Service requested
- Contact permission status
- Contact attempts
- Last contact date
- Assigned staff member
- Outcome
- Booked, pending, closed, or lost
- Notes requiring human review

### 5. Optional AI Support Layer

AI may be used to accelerate drafting and summarization, but it should not become the authoritative record.

Approved AI support uses:

- Draft first-pass review replies
- Summarize non-clinical call notes for staff review
- Rewrite staff responses into an approved tone
- Personalize dormant-lead email drafts from client-approved CRM data
- Generate executive summaries from validated tracking data

Required guardrails:

- AI outputs are drafts only.
- Client staff approve all external communications.
- Protected health information should not be entered into unapproved AI tools.
- Generated insights must be separated from source records.
- The final report must show which inputs were public, client-provided, or AI-assisted.

## Operating Rules

Use these rules to keep the sprint defensible for healthcare and regulated clients:

- Do not impersonate patients or customers.
- Do not create fake reviews or suggest review manipulation.
- Do not use scraped personal phone lists.
- Do not send automated SMS campaigns without documented consent.
- Do not enter protected health information into unapproved tools.
- Do not treat AI-generated text as approved client communication.
- Do not make clinical, legal, or compliance claims beyond the evidence reviewed.
- Do not hide assumptions. Label them clearly.
- Do not leave owners, dates, or next steps ambiguous.

## Scaling the Offer

After three clients in one niche, convert the sprint into a repeatable niche package.

Examples:

- Med Spa Lead Recovery System
- Dental Implant Inquiry Recovery Kit
- Chiropractic Appointment Recovery Sprint

The goal is a 70 percent template and 30 percent customization model. Keep the evidence review specific to each client, but standardize scripts, trackers, report structure, intake questions, and retainer summaries.

## Definition of Done

A sprint is complete only when the client receives:

- A one-page revenue leak report
- A prioritized fix list
- A script pack labeled as draft or approved
- A simple follow-up workflow
- A lead tracking sheet
- A documented human review checkpoint
- A retainer recommendation or close-out note

The final delivery should make the next action obvious to a busy operator and understandable to an executive, compliance officer, or auditor.
